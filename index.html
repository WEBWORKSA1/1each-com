Questions

What is 1Each?

1Each gives one clear answer for each everyday decision: a single best-value pick per category, free calculators that show the true cost of each option, and a fair split for every shared bill.

Is 1Each free?

Yes. Every guide and tool is free, with no sign-up. We're supported by ads, affiliate commissions, sponsors and reader donations — never by paid rankings.

How do you choose the one pick?

We start from a single decision rule per category, apply a checklist of must-haves, then give a budget, top and upgrade option. The math behind every tool is shown on the page.

Can I get a personal recommendation?

Yes — use Get Matched. Tell us your budget and must-haves and a person replies with one pick, usually within 48 hours.
