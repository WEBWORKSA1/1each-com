The "buy once" math

A $240 pair that lasts six years costs less per wear than a $60 pair replaced every year. Cost per use turns that instinct into a number — and also shows when the cheap option wins because you'll rarely use it.

FAQ

What counts as a use?

Anything you'd reasonably count: a wear, a ride, a cook, a session. Keep it consistent across both options.

Should I include resale value?

Yes, if you'd realistically sell it. Quality items often keep resale value, which lowers their true cost.
