## Lead-form patterns used
1. A privacy promise at the point of entry (Bankrate).
2. "Match" framing (TPG and Bankrate CardMatch).
3. A specific payoff: "your 1 pick within 48h" (DealNews, Uncrate).
4. Low-friction steps with the time stated, e.g. "60 seconds" (Buy Me a Coffee, GoFundMe).
5. Contest entries used as lead capture, with referral bonuses (Gleam).
